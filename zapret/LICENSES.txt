zapret (winws) - included binary component
============================================

This program bundles winws.exe from the zapret project, a separate work
by bol-van and contributors, distributed under the GNU General Public
License v3.0. It is NOT part of the VPN LAUNCHER source code and is
covered by its own license.

Source  : https://github.com/bol-van/zapret
License : https://github.com/bol-van/zapret/blob/master/LICENSE
Provenance: binaries taken from the zapret community repack v1.10.3
(community "general*.bat" strategy presets, converted to
vpn_launcher/core/zapret_presets.py).

The bundled payload packets (bin/*.bin: fake TLS ClientHello / QUIC
initial / STUN templates) and host/ip lists (lists/*.txt) come from the
same repack; they are data files used by winws command-line options.

WinDivert (https://github.com/reqrefuse/WinDivert), the packet capture
driver used by winws (bin/WinDivert.dll, bin/WinDivert64.sys), is
distributed under the LGPL-3.0 and is dynamically loaded by winws.
